LEARNING CENTRE

Mechanics lab

Speed & acceleration

Speed relates to top running pace. Acceleration relates to how quickly a player reaches that pace. Space, stamina and player movement also matter.

Balance & physical contact

Balance can influence how well a player stays composed while moving or being challenged. Physical contact is one part of shoulder-to-shoulder contests.

Dribbling & tight possession

Dribbling is useful for carrying the ball. Tight possession is especially relevant to close control and small movements.

Passing & through balls

Passing attributes contribute to pass quality. The passing lane, player orientation, pressure and pass command also matter.

Shooting & finishing

Finishing contributes to shot accuracy in scoring situations. Shot type, body position, pressure and distance can change the outcome.

Defending & awareness

Defensive awareness helps positioning. Match-up timing, player switching and team shape remain important.
